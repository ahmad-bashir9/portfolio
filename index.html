Exploring NeRF beyond COLMAP

2022

Bachelor thesis, ITU

My bachelor thesis. NeRF normally relies on COLMAP (Structure-from-Motion) to recover camera poses, which is slow and fragile. I built a lightweight hardware rig that captures calibrated poses directly, so the pipeline runs from calibration into NeRF and out to rendered views and a 3D mesh. It handles small, inwards-bounded objects, faster and cheaper than COLMAP.

Python, PyTorch, NeRF, custom capture hardware
